 KEY FINDINGS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. DEMAND > SUPPLY for crisis detection:
   - Demand features (demand_gw, ramp_rate, vol_6h, dev_24h, temp_stress) are
     STRONGER signals for WinterStorm Uri than supply features
   - Physics: Gravity on demand = 0.9955 vs Hybrid on supply = 0.7692
   - Canonical: FWS within-yr demand = 0.9960 vs FWS effAUC supply = 0.7660

2. COMBINED achieves best physics (Hybrid AUC=0.9902):
   - Adding supply features to demand moderately helps physics engines
   - But canonical FWS cross-year DEGRADES: supply drift dilutes demand signal

3. SUPPLY inversion insight:
   - WinterStorm Uri supply crisis (gas/coal collapse) resembles 2019 patterns
   - Normal 2021 supply DIFFERS MORE from 2019 than crisis 2021 (capacity growth)
   - Cross-year FWS inverts: normal=more novel, crisis=less novel vs 2019
   - Within-year supply detection is weak (effAUC≈0.58)

4. PAPER FRICTION (γ*(x)) effects:
   - Supply: slightly degrades (0.766→0.636 cross-yr)
   - Demand: degrades heavily (0.9960→0.613 within-yr)
   - Combined: mixed results (0.9800→0.8330 within-yr)
   - Raw FWS consistently outperforms its friction-modified variants

5. BSDT channels uniformly weak on ERCOT supply&combined (effAUC 0.56-0.58):
   - delta_A and delta_T show inverted separation on supply
   - delta_G (feature gap/sparsity) shows correct separation on demand
   - GeometricBSDT variants nearly identical to base BSDT

6. WinterStorm Uri detection timing:
   - FWS within-yr demand: lead=+881h (~37 days early), HR=0.996 ← exceptional
   - FWS cross-yr demand: lead=+809h (~34 days early), HR=0.604
   - Molecular supply: lead=+956h (~40 days early), HR=0.604
   - Physics combined (Gravity): lead=+306h (~13 days early), HR=0.350
