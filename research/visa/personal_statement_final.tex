\textbf{Innovation: The AMTTP Protocol}

To address the structural fragmentation in digital-asset compliance, I spent three years in sustained independent research and development to design and build the \textbf{Anti-Money Laundering Transaction Trust Protocol (AMTTP)}. Where conventional tooling acts post-settlement, AMTTP enables risk decisions \emph{before} transaction finality. As an \textbf{independent founder}, working without institutional sponsorship, venture funding, or a delivery team, I architected and delivered the entire system end to end---spanning a consumer-facing application, an enterprise War Room dashboard, a nine-service Python orchestrator, and the underlying smart-contract layer. This is not theoretical work: the platform is live on a Sepolia-backed environment at \textbf{\texttt{amttp.com}}, with public usage attested by independent Cloudflare analytics (\textbf{MC-1, OC3-2}). Holding sole technical leadership of a system of this scope, while it remained publicly operational, evidences a capacity to lead production-grade digital infrastructure at a level rarely demonstrated outside a funded company.

\textbf{Technical Contribution and Security Excellence}

My focus is the harder edge of compliance: privacy and verifiability. Through \textbf{zkNAF}, I integrated \textbf{Groth16} zero-knowledge proofs so that sanctions checks and KYC confirmations may be performed without exposing personal data on a public ledger. To establish institutional-grade resilience, I subjected the contract layer to \textbf{Slither} static analysis and \textbf{Echidna} property-based fuzzing at deep iteration sequences, and complemented this with a Hardhat integration suite covering deployment, transfer logic, and policy enforcement. These artefacts, together with the audited contract suite (\textbf{MC-3, OC3-4}), evidence engineering discipline well beyond the threshold expected at the Promise stage.
