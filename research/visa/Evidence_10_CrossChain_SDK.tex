\section*{1. Beyond a Single Chain: Cross-Chain Compliance}
A critical limitation of existing Web3 compliance tools is that they operate on a single blockchain. I designed AMTTP to be \textbf{chain-agnostic} from inception, with cross-chain bridging capabilities that allow the compliance engine to monitor and enforce policy across Ethereum, Polygon, Arbitrum, and other EVM-compatible networks simultaneously.

This architectural decision positions AMTTP as an infrastructure-layer protocol rather than a single-chain application, dramatically expanding its addressable market from one blockchain ecosystem to the entire multi-chain DeFi landscape.

\noindent\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.95\textwidth}

\texttt{~~~~UUPSUpgradeable, ILayerZeroReceiver \{}\\[4pt]

\section*{2. Developer SDKs: TypeScript \& Python}
To accelerate third-party adoption, I engineered and published two developer SDKs that abstract the complexity of the AMTTP protocol into simple, importable libraries:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{TypeScript SDK} (\texttt{@amttp/client-sdk}): Provides ML-powered risk scoring, smart contract integration (Policy Engine \& Manager), KYC compliance checking, real-time inference, atomic swap creation, and multi-chain support (Ethereum, Polygon, Arbitrum).
    \item \textbf{Python SDK} (\texttt{amttp}): Mirrors the TypeScript SDK capabilities for data science teams and backend integrators --- transaction risk scoring, policy management, and programmatic access to the compliance engine.
\end{itemize}
