%% ── MARKET GAP ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────

\textbf{AMTTP solves a real, unsolved market problem.}

The blockchain compliance market has a structural deficiency:
\textit{every existing solution operates after settlement}.
Chainalysis, Elliptic, and TRM~Labs---collectively valued at over
US\$10~billion---provide post-settlement forensics.  They flag; they do
not prevent.  For FCA- or FinCEN-regulated institutions, discovering
\textit{after the fact} that they processed an illicit transaction
creates regulatory liability no amount of alerting can eliminate.

Olusegun's Anti-Money Laundering Transaction Trust Protocol (AMTTP) is,
to the best of my knowledge, the only system that provides
\textbf{pre-settlement compliance enforcement} on blockchain
transactions.  It scores, classifies (Approve / Review / Escrow /
Block), and gates transactions at the protocol level within Ethereum's
12-second block window.  This is not incremental---it is a categorically
different value proposition.
