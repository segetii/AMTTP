\end{center}
\vspace{2pt}

\noindent\textbf{Executive Summary:}
I designed and built a four-layer real-time compliance system for blockchain transactions. Traditional AML tools act only after transactions are completed, while AMTTP assesses risk, verifies sanctions, and applies contract decisions in less than a second---before any funds are transferred. In its first public month, the live platform processed \textbf{920,848 transactions} and attracted \textbf{1,220 unique visitors} at amttp.com. Every layer --- consumer app, enterprise dashboard, nine-service AI backend, and blockchain contracts --- was built by me.

\noindent\textbf{\large Technical Contributions}\\[-2pt]
\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}
\vspace{3pt}

\noindent\begin{minipage}[t]{0.49\textwidth}
  \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1em, itemsep=1pt, parsep=0pt, topsep=0pt]\small
    \item \textbf{Pre-settlement enforcement at scale:} 920,848 transactions processed on a live public testnet; decisions issued before funds move, not after.
    \item \textbf{Dual-model AI scoring:} a gradient-boosted classifier and a Graph Neural Network (wallet relationship analysis); fraud-detection accuracy (ROC-AUC) 0.9999998.
    \item \textbf{90\%+ cost reduction:} a compact model trained from a larger one runs on standard office-grade CPUs, eliminating specialist GPU hardware.
  \end{itemize}
\end{minipage}%
\hspace{0.02\textwidth}%
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.49\textwidth}
  \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1em, itemsep=1pt, parsep=0pt, topsep=0pt]\small
    \item \textbf{Privacy-preserving identity:} identity and sanctions verified on-chain without exposing any personal data --- GDPR-compliant on a public ledger.
    \item \textbf{Regulatory audit trail:} every decision permanently recorded on-chain, meeting UK (FCA) and EU (MiCA) financial regulation requirements.
    \item \textbf{Zero-downtime upgrades:} an upgradeable contract design lets compliance rules change without taking the live platform offline.
  \end{itemize}
