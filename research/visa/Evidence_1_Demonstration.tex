\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.95\textwidth}
\small
\texttt{@app.post("/score")}\\
\texttt{async def score\_transaction(request: TransactionRequest):}\\
\texttt{~~~~features = extract\_features(request)}\\\texttt{~~~~tabular\_score = xgb\_model.predict\_proba(features)[:,1]}\\
\texttt{~~~~graph\_score = await memgraph.query\_risk(request.from\_addr)}\\
\texttt{~~~~combined = 0.7 * tabular\_score + 0.3 * graph\_score}\\
\texttt{~~~~return \{"risk\_score": combined, "action": get\_policy\_action(combined)\}}

\section*{4. Consumer Edge \& Decentralized Settlement}
The final tier involves the cross-platform consumer client (built in Flutter) routing safe transactions to EVM-compatible blockchains. Compliance checks are verified entirely on-chain without leaking private keys or PII.

\texttt{~~~~~~~~~~~~g1\_mulAccC(\_pVk, IC2x, IC2y, calldataload(add(pubSignals, 32)))}\\
