\noindent\textbf{Overview:}
The Anti-Money Laundering Transaction Trust Protocol (AMTTP) evaluates blockchain transactions \textbf{before settlement}, producing deterministic Approve / Review / Escrow / Block outcomes within Ethereum''s 12-second block window. In contrast to conventional blockchain compliance workflows that are largely post-transaction and forensic in nature, AMTTP is designed for execution-time risk control before final settlement. I am the \textbf{sole architect and developer} of all principal components: ML pipeline, Solidity contracts, the orchestrator and 9 specialist microservices, and both Flutter and Next.js frontends.
