\section*{1. Overview}
The Anti-Money Laundering Transaction Trust Protocol (AMTTP) is a real-time compliance system that evaluates blockchain transactions \textbf{before settlement}, producing deterministic outcomes (Approve, Review, Escrow, or Block) within Ethereum's 12-second block window. Existing tools --- including Chainalysis, Elliptic, and TRM Labs --- operate post-transaction and cannot prevent risk exposure before execution. AMTTP introduces pre-settlement compliance enforcement as a novel architectural approach.

I am the \textbf{sole architect and developer} of this system, independently designing and implementing all components across machine learning, blockchain infrastructure, compliance logic, and client integration.

\section*{4. Key Technical Innovations}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Pre-settlement enforcement} --- compliance decisions made within the transaction lifecycle, not after
    \item \textbf{Hybrid ML + graph scoring} --- XGBoost tabular combined with GraphSAGE graph analysis
    \item \textbf{Knowledge distillation pipeline} --- composite teacher model reduced to a lightweight student for real-time inference (see Evidence~6)
    \item \textbf{Privacy-preserving compliance} --- Groth16 ZK-SNARKs on BN254 prove compliance facts without exposing personal data
    \item \textbf{Deterministic decision matrix} --- Approve / Review / Escrow / Block with regulatory reproducibility
\end{itemize}
