COMMON QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: Should I edit the recommendation letter templates myself?
A: No. Share templates with recommenders and request they use them
   (or provide their own). You should not write your own recommendations.

Q: Can I copy content from the existing PDFs into the Word templates?
A: Yes! Use both the existing PDFs and templates to create stronger
   documents. Combine best content from each.

Q: What if I don't have all the numbers/metrics?
A: Use what you have and estimate conservatively if needed. Be honest
   about data availability in your write-ups.

Q: Do I need all 15 documents?
A: Minimum: Recommendation letters + MC1, MC2, MC3 = 5 documents
   Recommended: Add OC3/OC4 = 11 documents
   Ideal: Full package = 15 documents

Q: Can I submit PDFs or do they need to be Word docs?
A: Depends on visa portal. Check submission requirements.
   Most accept both PDF and Word. PDF is more formal for finished
   version, but editing in Word first is easier.

Q: Should I remove the [INSTRUCTIONS] text from templates?
A: YES. All instructions in [brackets] should be replaced or deleted.
   Only keep actual evidence content.

Q: How do I verify my evidence is strong enough?
A: Compare against: Independent validation? Commercial viability?
   Academic recognition? Original contribution? UK potential?
   If yes to all, evidence is strong.
