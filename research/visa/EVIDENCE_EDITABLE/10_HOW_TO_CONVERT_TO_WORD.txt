1. Find every [bracketed item]

Step 4: Add Specific Evidence

For each section, add:
- Real numbers (not placeholders)
- Actual URLs and links
- Dates of achievements
- Quotes or excerpts from supporting documents
- Examples specific to your work

Step 5: Professional Polish

1. Ensure consistent formatting
2. Check spelling and grammar
3. Make sure tone is formal but accessible
4. Verify no [brackets] remain accidentally
5. Proper paragraph spacing and indentation

Step 6: Save & Verify

1. Save as .docx (not .txt)
2. Check it opens correctly in Word
3. Verify formatting preserved
4. Read through once more for completeness

COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
================================================================================

❌ MISTAKE 1: Leaving [Brackets] Unfilled

Check: Search each document for "[" to find unfilled placeholders
Fix: Replace every [bracketed item] with actual information

---

❌ MISTAKE 2: Inconsistent Links/URLs

Check: Verify all GitHub URLs are correct and working
Fix: Test each URL before final submission

---

❌ MISTAKE 3: Placeholder Numbers Instead of Real Metrics

Example: "361+ commits" should be exact if available
Fix: Get real numbers from GitHub, metrics dashboards, etc.

---

❌ MISTAKE 4: Wrong Font or Formatting

Check: Ensure consistent font (e.g., Calibri 11pt throughout)
Fix: Select all and apply uniform formatting

---

❌ MISTAKE 5: Too Much or Too Little Detail

Check: section lengths reasonable for the category
Fix: Match detail level to other sections

---

❌ MISTAKE 6: Vague Language

Example: "Very good code" → "361 commits, 95% test coverage"
Fix: Use specific metrics and evidence

---

❌ MISTAKE 7: Not Removing Instruction Text

These templates include [INSTRUCTIONS] in brackets
Check: Delete all instruction text before final submission
Fix: Keep only the actual evidence content
