1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW
================================================================================

What is AMTTP?
AMTTP (Anti-Money Laundering Transaction Trust Protocol) is a blockchain-native 
compliance system designed to make compliance decisions before transaction 
settlement.

Key Innovation:
- Traditional compliance tools operate AFTER funds move
- AMTTP operates IN THE CONFIRMATION WINDOW (before settlement)
- Enables real-time approve/escrow/review/block decisions
- Privacy-preserving (zero-knowledge verification)
- Deployed on Ethereum Sepolia testnet (live, publicly accessible)

Core Capability:
Combines ML risk scoring, sanctions screening, relationship analysis, and policy 
evaluation to enable pre-settlement compliance decisions at scale.
