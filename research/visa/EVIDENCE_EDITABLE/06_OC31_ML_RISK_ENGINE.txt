Classification Metrics:

Confusion Matrix:
                 Predicted Fraud    Predicted Legitimate
Actual Fraud:        [TP]                   [FN]
Actual Legit:        [FP]                   [TN]

Precision: [TP/(TP+FP)] = [X%]
  → Of predicted frauds, how many are actually fraud?
  → High precision = low false alarms

Recall: [TP/(TP+FN)] = [X%]
  → Of actual frauds, how many are caught?
  → High recall = low missed detections

F1 Score: 2 * (Precision * Recall) / (Precision + Recall) = [X%]
  → Harmonic mean (balanced metric)

ROC-AUC:
  - Receiver Operating Characteristic
  - Area Under Curve = [0.9898 to 0.9999998]
  - Interpretation: [X%] probability model ranks random fraud > random legit

  - More sensitive to imbalanced datasets
  - Area Under Curve = [0.9997 to 0.9999998]
